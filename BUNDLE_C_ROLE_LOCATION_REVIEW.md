   native mass-peak cells. Shared native mass cells/peaks from343469 do not
   establish that SubhaloPos center labels are shared. Determine the relevant
   center-cell counts from the existing request inside preparation; never
   substitute historical peak statistics into the new mixture reference.
2. State q_theta(c|F,O,E_train), not an observed posterior or a new halo
   catalogue. Existence/observer selection is conditional and uncalibrated
   for the real LG. A nonzero categorical output on every field does not
   prove such a galaxy system physically exists there.
3. Teacher forcing is valid for joint log likelihood: all three normalized
   factors evaluated at a labelled tuple. It is NOT proof of autonomous
   identification. The separate64-triple-per-development rollout must never
   replace sampled parents with native parents. Log both kinds separately.
4. Exact symmetry is only the48 signed permutations of the grid. Scalar
   features use V norm and radial V/diagonal-variance contractions; indices,
   O and parents transform with the field. Cubic kernel orbit ties plus
   symmetric pooling/interpolation give the algebraic property. Verify it
   numerically before/after the fit; do not infer it from augmentation alone.
5. Freeze the density reference precisely: for each parent context,
   q_density(c) proportional to q_geometry(c)*(1+M(c)/mean_cell_mass).
   M is native total mass, mean over this cube. This is a probability
   reference, NOT an added physical-mass floor. The geometry reference uses
   the fixed .75/1.5/1.0 widths, normalized over the whole patch.
   M33 same-cell reference is pi*delta(c_T=c_A)+(1-pi)*q_density_T,
   with pi=(training shared-center count+1)/(13+2). It is never imported
   from mass-peak overlap and never estimated on development targets.
6. Keep all per-role/per-field scores, including training scores. A mean
   improvement over3 reused fields can be noisy and does not certify
   calibration. Sample spreads are model spreads, not measured coverage.
   Random-source multiplicity alone does not force a particular posterior
   width or prove errors in a unique-choice field are assignment failures.
7. A pass may justify adding/calibrating conditional mass/velocity state and
