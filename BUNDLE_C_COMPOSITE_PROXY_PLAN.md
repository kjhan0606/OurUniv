## Frozen low-dimensional statistical baseline

For a pair i,j, model four relative three-vectors in BOX-frame physical kpc
and peculiar km/s: r31, r32, v31, v32. Carrier predictions are
[(x_j-x_i), 0, (v_j-v_i), 0]. Host denotes the M31+M33 unresolved composite
proxy, NOT a declaration that its COM is precisely M31's stellar center.

Let e be the pair unit separation, length unit ell=1000*dx/h kpc. Normalize
the four residual rows by [ell,ell,s31,s32], where
s31=sqrt(100^2+mean(sigma_i^2)+mean(sigma_j^2)) km/s and
s32=sqrt(100^2+mean(sigma_j^2)) km/s. These are frozen positive feature scales;
100 km/s is a reference scale, NOT a measured physical dispersion or error
floor added to the field. Report saved physical sigma separately.

Residual mean in each normalized row is beta_g*e (four fitted coefficients).
The joint12D normalized residual covariance is K(4x4) tensor I(3), retaining
cross-role position/velocity correlations with ten free symmetric entries.
Fit beta and the pooled axis covariance once on TRAINING objects with fixed
10% diagonal covariance shrinkage. Positive definiteness required; no tuning
or invented covariance if the fit is singular. Use exact multivariate Gaussian
normalization including all scale determinants. Total14 fitted coefficients;
the isotropy/separable-axis approximation is an explicit strong limitation,
not39 independent universes or measured zero physical tensor cross terms.

Training may use native labels to associate the two HOST roles to frozen
candidate pairs, using the declared one-cell matching convention. Do not run
the earlier three-role competition that assigned a shared host peak to M33.
Report eligible unique host pairs, excluded associations and repeated members;
conditional calibration excludes failed associations and is not completeness.
Require at least8 distinct training pairs; otherwise stop without widening
matching radius. Evaluation uses the mixture, never truth-selected anchors.

Truth r/v target comes from native catalogue centers and massive-member COMs,
not from the aperture or an artificial Gaussian draw. Native masses are bound
SUBFIND members; M33 is the present stripped bound object. No M200c/bound-mass
conversion or actual mass likelihood is fitted in this kinematic baseline.
Gaussian position tails are not a calibrated physical satellite/selection law;
do not reject inconvenient draws and silently drop their normalization.

## One diagnostic job and useful output

One CPU fit/evaluation, no neural learner, no optimizer update ladder:

1. Fit on the frozen training subset, report14 parameters and source counts.
2. Evaluate the full pair-mixture log density at each of three retained native
   LG kinematic targets, not at data generated by this same Gaussian.
3. Compute the fixed3x3 table of those targets scored against all three retained
   field contexts. It probes dependence on F without a mean-field/shuffle outside
   the available source support. It is not calibrated information gain.
4. Report the MW–host marginal and conditional-M33 contribution separately:
   log q(all12 | F) - log q(r31,v31 | F). Marginalize the mixture first; this is
   not the average of per-pair conditional log densities. If total discrimination
   comes only from MW–host geometry, do not attribute it to M33.
5. One fixed noiseless coordinate/observable roundtrip checks BOX-to-declared
   mock ICRS/Solar/Hubble conventions. Keep resolved_halos guard unchanged;
   use a distinctly labelled latent-proxy adapter, never a fake resolved catalogue.
   The statistical test is the12D Cartesian density, not an8D likelihood at
   fixed sky with a missing sky-conditioning normalization/Jacobian.

Output one report with field-context score matrix, role/pair ambiguity and
native versus proxy relative-kinematic plots. Physical sigma and posterior
uncertainty remain distinct. Original samples are native calibration cases,
not actual CF4 or observed LG posterior realizations.

## Decision and boundary

Numerical completion is distinct from scientific adequacy. Three same-box
heldout objects cannot certify coverage, posterior accuracy or cosmic selection.
State whether host dependence and incremental M33 dependence are visible, weak
or absent; no mandatory positive score and no artificial success threshold.
If useful, next design may connect the diagnostic proxy to a properly specified
field prior/selection model. If not, report which missing dependence prevents
that connection; no automatic high-dimensional learning or extra simulations.

For actual inference, p(E|F,O) or an E-conditioned field prior is still missing,
q_F morphology is not validated, published covariance/stellar/LMC systematics
and actual CF4 overlap remain unresolved. Sharper M33 labels alone do not achieve
the project goal of an LG-conditioned density/velocity field.
