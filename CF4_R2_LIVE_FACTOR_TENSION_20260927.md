## Bounded question

The last bundle established a finite same-IC 2M++ count plus conditional CF4
mark gradient, but fixed ARES-derived nonlinear biases and inherited FoG are
not calibrated for this PMWD field. Before sampling this partial target,
measure whether the two data factors request concordant IC updates and how
strongly the count score changes under transparent perturbations of those
fixed response parameters. This is a model-stress test, **not** a posterior,
Bayes factor, scale-of-information claim, or a fitted bias/FoG calibration.

Use only the saved initial and two predetermined terminal IC/hyper/group states
from H100406356. They were generated by the CF4-mark-only development target,
not selected by the present count score. At each same state, compute the
gradient of the count and the CF4 mark with respect to all N128/384 whitened
IC coordinates, then their global and three predeclared Fourier-band norms
and cosines (k<=0.1, 0.1<k<=0.3, k>0.3 h/Mpc). The band edges are diagnostics,
not an estimated low/high-k boundary. Save scalar summaries only, no extra
field copies. At those same states, hold the state fixed and compare the
count log score under six-population published biases scaled together by
0.8/1.2 and inherited FoG widths scaled by 0.5/2. Neither scale range is a
prior or a physical uncertainty interval; the perturbations only reveal
whether fixed assumptions materially affect this development score. The
CF4 mark factor and source observations are otherwise unchanged.

Reuse the source-bound count keys, Q257 hierarchical geometry, integrated
selection, PMWD solver and conservative native-node/voxel conversion.
Check exact source ownership, finite factors/gradients, positive occupied
intensity and a small Parseval regression for the band bookkeeping. A
large score difference or opposing local gradients is a **warning about this
partial target**, not proof that the real CF4 and 2M++ observations are
physically inconsistent: source-group inclusion, nonlinear tracer bias,
RSD/FoG and source covariance remain uncalibrated. Do not use this diagnostic
to pick an IC seed, rescale a likelihood, or launch N256.

One H100 Slurm job, four CPUs, 10GiB host request (estimated <=8GiB peak plus
20% rounded), <=20min; no new PM simulation, posterior chain, downloaded
survey, full field or external audit. Preserve all prior results.

Q-GOAL: the calculation directly tests whether the newly joined actual
2M++ density and CF4 distance terms provide compatible *model-conditional*
directions on the same LCDM IC, especially over the R2 surroundings modes.
It informs the next observation-model/inference choice without pretending
that a single diagnostic reveals the actual z=0 map.

Q-LEAN: three existing saved states, four fixed response perturbations and
one Slurm calculation; no new gate ladder or short-chain extension.

MW/M31/M33 remain R3 latent identities from each **new** evolved state.
Their role law must retain MW/M31 ambiguity and resolved/shared/unresolved
M33 cases and apply their observed positions/velocities/masses to that same
state. Native truth identities may calibrate/evaluate but never seed or select
generated candidates. N128/3 cMpc/h fields here do not resolve these roles.

Decision: **NO-GO for freezing the transferred six bias exponents and FoG
widths in a production R2 sampler.** A longer chain using the same fixed
model would measure a conditional distribution whose density field can be
highly sensitive to those unsupported choices. The next useful work is a
joint nuisance/selection treatment constrained or stress-tested by external
source-matched mocks or independent information, retaining the CF4 group
conditional dependence and heldout ownership. Do not retune bias to maximize
these three saved-state scores or use the mark-only endpoints as calibration
data. N256 and <=0.3 LG inference remain on hold.
