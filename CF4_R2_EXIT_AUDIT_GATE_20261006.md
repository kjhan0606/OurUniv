the code and results, and continue from the advice that the evidence supports.
Record any part that is amended or rejected, with the reason. Do not send the
exit email. Do not enter R3. Do not answer a withheld approval by inventing a
selection correction, reading held-out early, or repeating a failed optimizer
cap.

A later exit attempt requires a new Astra audit after the continued work is
recorded. An earlier rejection is not reused as approval.
